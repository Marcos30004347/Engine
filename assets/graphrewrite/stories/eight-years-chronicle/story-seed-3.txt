-- August --

Ceolwulf Ceolring declared for Bertwald Wulfing.

The season went badly for the house of Aldring.

Elsewhere that month: a plot laid.

-- September --

Bertwald Wulfing, sworn though he was, took the throne of Wessex for himself. The upshot was that Dunstan Wulfing was named heir to Wessex. Which is why Cenric Aldring wanted the crown of Wessex, and resolved that Bertwald Wulfing should not keep it. Cenric Aldring said, "Bertwald Wulfing sits where I should sit. That is the whole of it."

Maerwyn Aldring learned it was Bertwald Wulfing who had killed Aldric Aldring, and swore he would pay for it.

Wighelm Ceolring drowned in the river; it was Hereward Ceolring who was there. And so the wedding of Godgifu Godring and Wighelm Ceolring, set for late November, never took place. Godgifu Godring said, "The day is still marked. There will be nobody at it."

Elsewhere that month: a move on the crown, a friendship, a plot laid, three quarrels and one smaller matter.

-- October --

Bertwald Wulfing could not carry it alone, and told Father Oswin what he had done to Aldric Aldring; Father Oswin heard him out, and laid a penance on him that had mid-January of the following year at the end of it. Bertwald Wulfing said plainly to Father Oswin, "Is there a penance for it?" Father Oswin replied to Bertwald Wulfing, "There is. It runs until mid-January of the following year, and it will not feel like enough."

Maerwyn Aldring found Cenric Aldring, who had no love for Bertwald Wulfing, willing to help. Not long after, Dunstan Wulfing heard Maerwyn Aldring through the wall, and knew what she intended. Which is why Maerwyn Aldring lost her nerve and warned Bertwald Wulfing what Cenric Aldring intended; forewarned, Bertwald Wulfing resolved to strike first. Maerwyn Aldring said to Bertwald Wulfing, "I have no stomach for it. Cenric Aldring is coming." And so Cenric Aldring lost his nerve and warned Bertwald Wulfing what Maerwyn Aldring intended.
